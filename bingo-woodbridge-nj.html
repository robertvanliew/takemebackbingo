The Local Night

Where music bingo lives in Woodbridge.

Woodbridge Township is where Take Me Back Bingo runs its home nights. We’re a hip‑hop and R&B music bingo brand out of Middlesex County, and Lake Chateau Banquets on Route 9 is our home venue. Roughly monthly, we take over the room for a 21+ night: DJ Tameil dropping 30‑second clips, five‑in‑a‑row wins, cash prizes every round, dance battles and lip sync wars in between.

If you’ve been looking for music bingo near me, bingo in Woodbridge, or a Saturday night bingo NJ that doesn’t look like a church basement — this is it. Real DJ. Real prizes. Real dance floor.

Public nights sell out. We recommend early bird tickets.

What to Expect

Doors, drinks, three rounds, a dance floor.

  1. Doors around 6 PM (evening nights) or 3 PM (day parties). Full bar. Bring your ID. Grab your card at the door — that's your seat for the night.
  2. Show starts an hour after doors. Host warms up the room, DJ Tameil settles in. Round one drops early enough that you're still eating.
  3. Three rounds. Cash prize each round. Dance battles and lip sync wars between rounds. Nobody sits still by round two.
  4. After-party runs until 12 AM. The card game ends but the DJ keeps going. Stay, dance, meet everyone at the table you didn’t know before the first round.

FAQ

Common questions from the neighborhood.

  • Where exactly is Lake Chateau Banquets?

    1002 US‑9, Woodbridge Township, NJ 07095. Right on Route 9 between the Turnpike exit and the mall. Free lot on site.

  • Is there food?

    Full menu at the venue — sit‑down service. You order at your table before the show. Ticket doesn’t include food unless a specific event says so.

  • How do I get here from New York?

    Turnpike South to exit 11, then Route 9 south for a few miles. About 40 minutes from Manhattan off‑peak. Rideshare works too.

  • Is it 21+?

    Yes. Public nights at Lake Chateau are 21+ per the venue license. Bring physical ID.

  • How often do you run nights in Woodbridge?

    Roughly monthly. Check /events for the current calendar. Nights sell out — buy early.

  • Do you run bingo in other NJ cities?

    Woodbridge is the home venue for public events. Private bookings run across NJ and NYC — we bring the host, DJ, cards, and dauber setup to your venue.
