How to Use This

  1. Save your GBP review link at the top — only need to do this once.
  2. Pick a past attendee from your phone contacts or Instagram DMs.
  3. Type their first name above — the message auto-personalizes.
  4. Click SMS or WhatsApp — opens your messaging app with the message pre-typed. Or click Copy Message to paste anywhere.
  5. Send it. Repeat for 10 past attendees this week.

Rule of thumb: reviews come easiest right after an event when the vibe is fresh. Send review requests within 48 hours of a night.
