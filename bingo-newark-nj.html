The Newark-Adjacent Night

Newark has clubs. Newark has bars. Nobody had music bingo — until now.

If you’re in Newark searching for “bingo near me”, you already know the truth — there isn’t much to find inside the city. The scene is nightclubs and church halls. What Newark doesn’t have is a recurring, DJ‑led, hip‑hop and R&B music bingo night.

Take Me Back Bingo runs exactly that at Lake Chateau Banquets in Woodbridge Township — the next town over, roughly 20 minutes down the Turnpike. It’s a straight shot from Downtown Newark, closer than most people realize.

The nights are 21+. Doors open early (usually 6 PM, or 3 PM for our Sunday day parties). The DJ drops 30‑second clips of throwback hip‑hop and R&B — from 80s Whitney to 2000s Beyoncé — and if the song’s on your card, you daub it. Five in a row wins the round. Cash prizes every round. Dance battles between.

Get Directions

From Downtown Newark: NJ Turnpike South to exit 11, then Route 9 south for a few miles. Off‑peak: 20 minutes. Rush hour: 30–35.

From Ironbound / East Ward: Same route, add ~5 min for local streets to Turnpike entrance.

From Weequahic / South Ward: Route 22 to the Parkway south, or straight down Route 27. About 25 minutes.

Public transit? Not easy — the venue is on a highway. Rideshare from Newark runs about $30–40 one way. If you’re coming as a group of 4+, that splits down fine.

Parking: Free on‑site lot at Lake Chateau. Get there before doors if you can.

FAQ

Common questions from the Newark side.

  • Do you run events actually IN Newark?

    Public events run in Woodbridge (~20 min south). Private bookings run everywhere in Essex County including Newark itself — Ironbound restaurants, private homes, and event spaces we’ve worked before.

  • Is it worth the drive from Newark?

    Every public night has sold out. If music bingo is what you’re looking for, this is the only recurring hip‑hop / R&B version in the immediate area. It’s a straight shot down the Turnpike.

  • Can I organize a group from Newark?

    Yes — groups of 6+ get their own reserved table. Ask about a group of 6+ when you book.

  • Cash prizes?

    Every round. Three rounds per night. Amounts scale with attendance.

  • How often do you run public nights?

    Roughly monthly. Check /events for the current calendar. Sells out early — grab tickets when the night drops.
