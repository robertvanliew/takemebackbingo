Why Music Bingo

A birthday party that isn't just dinner and a cake.

Milestone birthdays are hard. Nobody wants to plan trivia (the introverts hate it). Nobody wants karaoke (three people hog the mic). Bottle service at a nightclub means you spend $2,000 and don't hear a single conversation. Dinner and a cake is what your parents throw when you turn eight.

Music bingo splits the difference. Everyone has a card — even the friend who doesn't dance. Everyone plays — even the co‑worker who came because she felt obligated. The birthday person is at the center of the room the whole night, and the songs are literally from the era they grew up in.

And prizes at every round means the night has actual stakes, not just polite party small talk.

How It Works

Three rounds. Their era. Their music. Their night.

  1. You tell us the era. Turning 30? 2000s R&B and hip‑hop pool. Turning 40? 90s heat. Turning 50? 80s dance floor. Or a mix if their taste crosses decades. We build the pool around what they'll actually recognize — not what a playlist algorithm thinks they'd like.
  2. We show up with everything. Host on the mic, DJ with full sound rig, printed cards for every guest, daubers, prizes. You send the address, the count, and any specific songs (their wedding song, first dance, high school prom track). Setup takes 30 minutes.
  3. Three rounds. Birthday person's card wins are amplified. Their card is the focus — every time they get close, the host works the room. Between rounds: dance battles, lip sync wars, sing‑alongs. By round three the whole room is standing.

Included

  • Host on the mic — keeps the room engaged, works the birthday person into every big moment.

FAQ

What people ask before they book.

  • Can the whole song pool be from one artist?

    Yes. Beyoncé night, Jay‑Z night, MJ tribute — we've done all‑single‑artist pools. Just tell us the artist and how many tracks you want in rotation (typically 40–60).

  • How far in advance to book?

    Weekends 4–6 weeks out. Holidays (NYE weekend, July 4, etc.) 8+ weeks. Weeknights work with 2 weeks' notice most of the time.

  • Can we do this in a small apartment?

    If we have ~10 feet of clear space for the DJ setup and enough seating for the group, yes. Below ~15 guests, apartments work fine.

  • Is there a minimum group size?

    Nothing hard, but under 8 people the game format loses its energy. We recommend 10+.

  • Do you handle prizes?

    Either. We can source generic prizes or you supply. Birthday person's biggest prize is usually something meaningful — that's on you.

  • Can kids play?

    Private events: yes. Song pool can be adjusted for family‑friendly (skip the harder tracks). We've done sweet 16s, 21sts, and mixed‑generation family birthdays.
